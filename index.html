Safety

Lye (sodium and potassium hydroxide) is highly caustic and can cause severe burns and blindness. Wear safety goggles, gloves and long sleeves, work in a ventilated room away from children and pets, and always add lye to water, never water to lye. Use a scale that weighs to 1 g and check every recipe with a second lye calculator before you make it. Lathera's saponification values are typical values, cross-checked against several public sources; real oils and lye vary. All information is provided without guarantee. Never leave a burning candle unattended, and test-burn every new candle. Lathera does not recommend wicks.

Selling soap and scented candles is regulated in many countries (for example safety assessments and warning labels). Lathera's labels and ingredient lists are a starting point, not a legal check.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Lathera ("the app") is developed by Jonas Volk, Switzerland. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

No data collection

Lathera does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter – recipes, batches, photos, test burns, materials, prices and your own oils – is stored only on your device. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your backup under Apple's privacy policy. You can delete all data in the app (Settings → Delete all data); deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Photos

If you add a photo to a batch, you pick it with the system photo picker. Lathera only receives the photo you choose and stores it on your device.

PDF files

Recipe sheets and labels are created on your device. They only leave your device if you choose to share them, and then only to the recipient you pick.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, cure reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple shares only anonymous, aggregated sales information with developers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
